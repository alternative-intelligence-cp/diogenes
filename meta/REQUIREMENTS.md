# Diogenes: requirements

Drafted 2026-10-10, revised the same day against the critique [c1], the measurements M1–M5 and the author's follow-up. Each requirement is one testable sentence with a priority, its source, and how it will be verified.

- **Priority** follows the author's horizons: **P0** delegation across accounts and the Team seat, **P1** Gemini via Antigravity, **P2** a GPT auditor, **P3** devTeam and later. Priorities taken from [r8] keep its value unless a change is stated with a reason.
- **Source** gives [r8]'s R-number where the requirement comes from the author's practice, and the digest, observation or measurement that shows the requirement is possible.
- **★ marks the P0 slice**: the subset of P0 built in cycle 0.0, as small as possible. Other P0 requirements land in 0.1 and 0.2 (see ROADMAP).
- UNVERIFIED marks are kept where a requirement rests on a claim no digest confirmed. **Measured** marks M1–M5 where a measurement settled it.
- Identifiers are stable. DR-99 onward were appended in the revision; none has been dropped.

## A. Messaging

- **DR-1 (P0) ★** A send writes a durable record to the store, with a sequence number, before any delivery attempt is made. Source: R-1 [r8]; possible by any store [r4 §6]. Verify: send to a seat that is killed before its next turn; the message is still listed by `dio inbox` for that seat after restart.
- **DR-2 (P0) ★** A receiver acknowledges a message with one call, and the sender can query the message's state as `stored`, `delivered` or `acked`. Source: R-2 [r8]; a `SendMessage` result only means queued [r1b §1]. Verify: `dio ack m1` from the receiver; `dio sent --state` from the sender shows `acked` with the time.
- **DR-3 (P0) ★** Delivery into a Claude Code session works with one small hook script on `UserPromptSubmit`, and the same script on `PostToolUse` emitting only when the unread or reported count has changed since its last injection, so a seat in a long dispatching turn sees reports before its next prompt. Source: R-3 [r8]; `additionalContext` on those events [r1 §1.1]; [c1 C-20]. Verify: with only these two hook entries configured, a sent message appears as "1 unread" at the receiver's next turn, and a report posted mid-turn appears after the next tool call, once (DX-2).
- **DR-4 (P0) ★** The documentation and the hook text state that delivery happens at the receiver's next turn or tool call, and no component wakes an idle session unless that seat has started its own `dio wait` (DR-105). Source: R-4 [r8]; the author's rule [r8 §3.8]. Verify: read the shipped hook description; a seat with no `dio wait` receives nothing while idle in a 10-minute watch.
  - **Amended 2026-10-15 by cycle 0.0-min's decision 2, approved by the author:** two wakes are permitted, the seat's own `dio watch` and a native message from a peer session that `dio who` lists; Diogenes itself still wakes nothing (`roadmap/0.0/MIN.md`).
- **DR-5 (P0) ★** A message passes between two sessions on different accounts with no use of `SendMessage` and no shared `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR`. Source: R-5 [r8]; a hub is the shared point [r1 §2.5]. Verify: both accounts with `permissions.deny: ["SendMessage"]` for the test; the exchange still completes.
  - **Amended 2026-10-15 by cycle 0.0-min's decision 1(c), approved by the author:** the bar is no dependence on `SendMessage`, with the inbox as the proof. `SendMessage` is the default path; the file inbox is the required fallback, proved in M4 as one exchange each way with `SendMessage` denied on both sides, which meets the verify line above for one exchange (`roadmap/0.0/MIN.md`).
- **DR-6 (P0) ★** Every message is delivered inside an envelope that names the sender identity, the trust level and the authentication it carries, labelled as data and not as an instruction. Source: R-6 [r8]; the existing `<cross-session-message>` wrapper [r1 §2.1]; [r5 §5]. Verify: inspect the injected text and the `dio inbox` output for the envelope fields.
- **DR-7 (P0) ★** A message carries an authentication field (`digest` against a known baseline, `nonce`, or `none`) that the receiver checks against content it already holds; the hub never fills it from a sender-asserted name and never prints a nonce value, and in 0.0 the field is shown `unchecked` unless the receiver checks it by content. Source: R-7 [r8]; [c1 C-9]. Verify: a landing notice's digests are shown for the receiver to check; the envelope shows `auth: pass|fail|unchecked` and never a nonce string.
- **DR-8 (P1)** A message is immutable once sent, a reply names the message it answers, and a correction is a new message. Source: R-8 [r8]. Verify: `dio edit` does not exist; `dio reply m1` sets `in_reply_to = m1`; the store has no UPDATE path on message bodies.
- **DR-9 (P0)** A message addressed to a subagent routes to its spawning seat, or is refused with an explicit `not reachable` answer. Source: R-9 [r8]; subagents have no inbox of their own (firsthand §1). Verify: `dio send nitpick-libs_18/a1b2` lands in `nitpick-libs_18`'s inbox tagged `for: a1b2`; a send to an unknown agent id returns `not reachable`. Lands in 0.1.
- **DR-10 (P0) ★** A send states `no-reply-needed`, `reply-expected` or `ack-only`, and the store tracks which replies are owed. Source: R-10 [r8]. Verify: `dio owed` lists messages marked `reply-expected` that have no reply.
- **DR-11 (P0) ★** A message can be addressed to a role (`libs-seat`, `compiler-seat`), and the role resolves to its assigned holder at delivery time. Source: R-11 [r8]; DR-106. Verify: assign `libs-seat` to `_18` then to `_19`; a send to `libs-seat` reaches `_19`; a self-registration by a third session claiming `libs-seat` receives nothing.
  - **Amended 2026-10-15 by cycle 0.0-min's decision 3, approved by the author:** addressing by role is `dio who --address <role>`, which resolves the role to its holder from the committed role line, `meta/ROLES.md` (committed on `main` by `diogenes_0`); a session that asserts a role is not stopped, only ignored by the resolver. Enforcement is 0.1 (`roadmap/0.0/MIN.md`).
- **DR-12 (P0)** A send can target a logging sink instead of the working seat, with a `do-not-interrupt` flag that suppresses injection. Source: R-12 [r8]. Verify: a message to `libs-sink` is stored and listed but never counted in a working seat's unread injection.
- **DR-13 (P1, raised from [r8]'s P2 because the file inbox is the Gemini fallback)** Messages are exportable as one Markdown file per message, named `YYYY-MM-DDTHHMM-<sender>-<subject>.md`, so a harness that can only read files participates unchanged. Source: R-13 [r8]; every harness can read a file [r3 §5]. Verify: `dio export --inbox gemini` writes files that the existing Gemini convention accepts.
- **DR-14 (P0) ★** `dio inbox --since <seq>` is idempotent and returns messages in sequence order, so a lost notification is recovered by re-reading from the last acknowledged cursor. Source: [r3 §What this means, 5]. Verify: call it twice with the same cursor; identical output; delete the notification, re-read, nothing lost.
- **DR-15 (P1)** The hub enforces per-sender size and rate limits and refuses identical repeats within a short window. Source: Claude Code's own limits [r1b §1]; DoS mitigation [r5 §5]. Verify: 100 sends in one second from one identity; the excess is refused with a reason.

## B. Presence and roster

- **DR-16 (P0) ★** Presence records, per session, a role where one is assigned, an executor kind, a session id, a pid and its process start time, the harness-exported socket path, a current name, a `ListAgents` ref where one exists, an account, a harness, and a state of `live`, `live-idle`, `parked-on-purpose`, `quota-paused`, `unknown` or `gone`. Source: R-14 [r8]; registration at `SessionStart` [r1 §B.2]; [c1 C-10]. Verify: `dio who --json` shows every field for two registered sessions on two accounts.
- **DR-17 (P0) ★** `gone` requires positive evidence (a `SessionEnd` record, a closed-by-owner record, or a failed process check after a stated grace), `live-idle` is given when the process check passes but no heartbeat or call is recent, and silence with no possible process check yields `unknown`. Source: R-15 [r8]; [c1 C-10]. Verify: leave a registered session idle for longer than the grace; it reads `live-idle`; kill it; it reads `gone` after the grace.
- **DR-18 (P0)** A rename or a ref change is recorded as an event linking the old and new identity, and routing by role is unaffected. Source: R-16 [r8]; refs change at restart (firsthand §3). Verify: `dio rename` then `dio send @role` still delivers; `dio history <role>` shows the link.
- **DR-19 (P0)** The hub issues a nonce for a session, stores only its SHA-256, and checks a nonce against the transcripts under every configured config dir, reporting the number of transcripts in which it appears without ever printing the value. Source: R-17 [r8]; a nonce was found in exactly one of 494 transcripts (firsthand §3); [c1 C-9]. Verify: `dio nonce issue` prints the value once to the caller only; `dio nonce check` returns `found in 1 transcript: <path>`; the store holds no plaintext nonce. Lands in 0.1; the check stays manual in 0.0, as today.
- **DR-20 (P0) ★** Registration accepts a session id supplied by the seat or a hook and records how it was obtained (`hook-stdin`, `transcript-path`, `scratchpad-path`, `status-line`, `socket-map`). Source: R-18 [r8]; `CLAUDE_SESSION_ID` is empty in Bash (firsthand §3); hooks receive `session_id` [r1 §1.2]. Verify: register from a hook and from Bash; both rows carry the `how` field.
- **DR-21 (P1)** A paused seat can register a wake condition, such as a time or a meter reset, and the roster shows "resumes at T". Source: R-19 [r8]. Verify: `dio pause --until 2026-10-14T20:00Z`; `dio who` shows it.
- **DR-22 (P0)** The roster can be displayed as the rolling pool: working seat, designated successor, spare, logging sink, per project, with Team worker sessions listed separately as executors. Source: R-20 [r8]. Verify: `dio who --pool nitpick-libs` prints the four slots and `empty` where none, then the workers.
- **DR-23 (P1)** A seat's heartbeat is published without a model turn, from the `Stop` hook or the status-line script, and a heartbeat older than the grace moves the state to `live-idle` or `unknown` as DR-17 says. Source: [r1 §C]; the status line runs on each update (firsthand §3). Verify: watch `dio who` while a seat idles; its `last_seen` updates with no prompt.

## C. Wake and delivery

- **DR-24 (P0) ★** Injected context is a pointer and a count, under 1,000 characters, never a message or task body, and the receiver pulls bodies with `dio inbox` and `dio task list`. Source: [r1 §A.1]; caps of 10,000 characters in Claude Code and 2,500 in Codex [r3 §What this means, 3]. Verify: send a 50 KB message; the injection is under 1,000 characters and the body arrives through `dio inbox`.
- **DR-25 (P1)** A seat can opt in to one documented wake mechanism per harness beyond `dio wait` (Claude Code: an `asyncRewake` hook, a mod `$.prompt.submit`, or a cron fire), and no seat is woken without that opt-in. Source: R-68 [r8]; `asyncRewake` exit 2 wakes an idle session [r1b §3]; a mod's `$.prompt.submit` "waits until the session is idle and then starts a new turn" [r1b §6]; re-arm and ceiling UNVERIFIED (DX-11, DX-12). Verify: with opt-in, a sent message starts a turn in an idle session within the long-poll timeout; without, nothing happens.
- **DR-26 (P1)** Wake text is a pointer ("n unread from X; 1 task reported; call `dio inbox`"), never a body. Source: [r1 §A.1]. Verify: inspect the system reminder produced by the wake.
- **DR-27 (P2)** When native `SendMessage` is used between Claude sessions, the exchange is mirrored into the store for audit, without consuming it. Source: a mod's `session.receive` and `session.send` events [r1b §6]. Verify: a native message appears in `dio audit` with `transport: native`.
- **DR-28 (P1)** The roster shows, per session, which delivery modes it supports: `poll`, `inject`, `wait`, `wake`. Source: the capability matrices [r2 §4, r3 §5]. Verify: `dio who` prints the modes, and a send to a `poll`-only session reports `will be read at its next poll`.
- **DR-29 (P1)** The file-inbox directories are kept in sync as a projection of the store, so the hub being down does not stop a file-only harness. Source: [r3 §What this means, 2]; R-13 [r8]. Verify: stop the hub; a file written to the Gemini inbox is ingested when the hub returns; a message sent while up appears as a file within 5 s.

## D. Locks, claims and leases

- **DR-30 (P0) ★** A lease is one record with exactly one holder, acquired by an atomic compare-and-set on the authoritative store, and released only by the holder or by a recorded takeover. Source: R-21 [r8]; `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` in SQLite or a Lua script in Redis [r4 §1, §3]. Verify: two processes in different namespaces (one on the host, one in a background sandboxed Bash call) race for one lease in contested rounds; exactly one wins each round; a release by a non-holder is refused (DX-1 as revised).
- **DR-31 (P0) ★** Release is one operation that clears the holder and bumps the fencing token together, and the release announcement is generated from that operation. Source: R-22 [r8]. Verify: `dio lock release` prints the announcement with the new token; no separate "announce" command exists.
- **DR-32 (P0) ★** Acquisition is refused unless the lease reads free on the authoritative store at the moment of the compare-and-set, and no local or cached read is consulted. Source: R-23 [r8]. Verify: hold a lease in process A; process B with a stale copy is refused.
- **DR-33 (P0) ★** An absent lease and a free lease are distinct states, and neither permits a write without a take. Source: R-24 [r8]. Verify: `dio lock status` on an unknown id prints `absent`; the guard treats `absent` as not held.
- **DR-34 (P0) ★** A lease matches by exact id only. Source: R-25 [r8]; the devTeam substring defect. Verify: a lease on `me` is not matched by a check for `names`.
- **DR-35 (P0)** `dio lock status` reports, for every tree the lease governs, dirty and ahead/behind counts, and prints the denominator, with the set of trees discovered from the project's recorded checkouts rather than listed by hand. Source: R-26 [r8]. Verify: with eight trees, the output says `8 trees` and lists each.
- **DR-36 (P0) ★** A lease carries a TTL, an absolute expiry and a fencing token that increments on every take, and an expired lease is not held. Source: Agent Mail TTL leases [r6 §2]; crash release [r1 §C]. Verify: take with `--ttl 60s`; after 61 s, `status` reads `expired`; the next take has a higher token.
- **DR-37 (P1)** A claim records the work unit, the agent label, the start time and the model, and a claim whose agent cannot be shown live reads `stale` and triggers recovery. Source: R-27 [r8]. Verify: claim, kill the agent, `dio claims` shows `stale`.
- **DR-38 (P1)** A claim carries a declared write scope, and a claim whose scope intersects a live claim is refused before dispatch. Source: R-28 [r8]; devTeam P-12. Verify: two claims on overlapping globs; the second is refused with the overlap named.
- **DR-39 (P0) ★** Leases are keyed by project and layer and resource, never inferred from a directory name that can collide. Source: R-29 [r8]. Verify: three checkouts named `nitpick-posix` map to three distinct lease ids.
- **DR-40 (P3)** The holder, release and takeover history of a lease is queryable as one audit view. Source: R-30 [r8]. Verify: `dio lock history <id>`.
- **DR-41 (P1)** A `PreToolUse` guard denies Edit, Write and Bash in a governed tree when the session does not hold the lease, attributes subagent calls by `agent_id`, and fails closed when the hub is unreachable. Source: hook fields and `onFailure: "block"` [r1 §C]; fail-closed `agy` hooks [r2 §2.5]. Verify: DX-9.

## E. Boards and state

- **DR-42 (P0)** Every state record has one writer, and other agents submit proposals that the writer lands. Source: R-31 [r8]. Verify: a write by a non-writer is refused and stored as a proposal.
- **DR-43 (P0)** A convention, a check's reference value or a baseline is stored as a value with its procedure, in a queryable record. Source: R-32 [r8]. Verify: `dio fact get <key>` returns both the value and the command that re-derives it.
- **DR-44 (P1)** Event-like state records are append-only; a correction is a new record that supersedes the old one. Source: R-33 [r8]; Graphiti's invalidate-not-delete [r7 §4]. Verify: no UPDATE path; `superseded_by` is set.
- **DR-45 (P0)** A published count carries its denominator and exemptions. Source: R-34 [r8]. Verify: a count without a denominator is refused by the CLI.
- **DR-46 (P1)** State exposes `blocked`, `done`, `in flight` and `blocked-by` per work unit. Source: R-35 [r8]; A2A task states as vocabulary [r6 §5]. Verify: `dio board` shows the fields.
- **DR-47 (P2)** Verification before release is a recorded step with the verifier's evidence attached. Source: R-36 [r8]. Verify: a release without a verification row is refused.

## F. Handoffs

- **DR-48 (P0)** A handoff is a structured record with required fields: lock state, claims, open tasks and owed items, state as values, the peer roster, and the commands that re-verify each claim. Source: R-37 [r8]. Verify: `dio handoff brief` refuses a brief missing any field. Lands in 0.1.
- **DR-49 (P0)** The two-signal close is two distinct recorded events, `done-with-you` from the successor and `safe-to-close` from the outgoing seat, and silence stands in for neither. Source: R-38 [r8]. Verify: `dio handoff status` shows both signals with times or `pending`. Lands in 0.1.
- **DR-50 (P0)** The handoff tells each peer the new address once, moves the role by a recorded assignment, and records who was told and who acknowledged. Source: R-39 [r8]; DR-106. Verify: `dio handoff notify` sends `ack-only` messages and `dio handoff status` lists acks.
- **DR-51 (P0)** The system warns at the author's thresholds (80 % and 90 % context) and offers handoff-before-compaction as one action. Source: R-40 [r8]; the existing PostToolUse warning. Verify: a seat crossing 80 % gets the warning once; `dio handoff start` is suggested.
- **DR-52 (P1)** Anything held only in a session scratchpad can be moved into shared state at handoff, and a subagent's in-flight output is flagged `dies with the session`. Source: R-41 [r8]. Verify: `dio handoff stash <path>` stores it; the brief lists live subagents with the flag.
- **DR-53 (P1)** A peer returning after a silence longer than the grace is re-authenticated by content before its messages are trusted. Source: R-42 [r8]. Verify: a returning seat's first message is shown `auth: re-check required` until a nonce check passes.
- **DR-54 (P2)** A successor on a quota-exhausted model is detectable before the handoff. Source: R-43 [r8]. Verify: `dio handoff start` warns when the successor's account meter is at its limit.

- **DR-61 (P2)** An environment event (an earlyoom kill, exit 137 or 143) is recorded as such, distinct from a result. Source: R-50 [r8]. Verify: a background task killed with 137 shows `environment: killed`.
- **DR-62 (P2)** Per-agent tokens and wall time are logged so they can be set against tenure length. Source: R-51 [r8]. Verify: `dio ledger --by-seat`.

- **DR-68 (P1)** Every new entry starts `quarantined` and `unverified`, only a verifier whose identity differs from the producer can promote it, and default lookups never return quarantined entries as hits.
